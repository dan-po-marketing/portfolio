AI SDR for Reply Qualification & Follow-Up

A multi-agent AI system that handles the SDR function across email and LinkedIn, reading replies, qualifying through conversation, updating the CRM, and following up.

Role
Solo builder
Where
TimelinesAI · WhatsApp/CRM software
Tools
n8n · OpenAI · LangChain · Unipile · Close CRM · HeyReach · Slack

Situation

The demand generation engine produced a steady stream of replies. Every one needed classification, qualification, a contextual response, CRM updates, and follow-up scheduling. One person doing that at volume was the bottleneck, and hiring SDRs is slow and expensive. The question: could AI own the whole function, not just draft replies, but qualify, route, and follow up?

The decision

Protected sales time by letting AI handle reply triage, qualification, and follow-up, escalating only demo-ready leads to humans.

The system

  1. 1

    Categorize

    Classifies each reply into interested, not interested, bad fit, later, or uncertain, each with explicit rules.

  2. 2

    Detect demo intent

    Checks whether the lead wants a live demo or gave a referral. If so, it escalates to a human in Slack with context, so only demo-ready leads reach people.

  3. 3

    Orchestrate

    Coordinates a knowledge-base agent (queries product docs for pricing, features, integrations) and a drafting agent that writes the response from conversation context.

  4. 4

    Qualify

    Tracks CRM used and team size across turns. Qualified leads get a booking link and CRM update; smaller ones get a trial; unknowns get qualification questions woven in naturally.

  5. 5

    Verify & send

    Formats clean HTML, replies in-thread, logs the interaction, and schedules an escalating follow-up cadence that cancels itself when a lead's status changes.

Results

  • Handled 1,000+ interactions per month across email and LinkedIn with no human drafting
  • Produced half the company's sales meetings
  • Sub-minute response time vs. hours or days from a human SDR
  • CRM-aware sending, so it never emails closed or converted leads, auto-updates status

Under the hood

n8n, OpenAI (5 specialized agents), LangChain, Unipile, Close CRM, HeyReach, Slack
