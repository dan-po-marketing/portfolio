Self-Updating CRM & Attribution System

A system where the CRM updates itself: product events sync statuses in real time, demos auto-analyze into notes, and attribution reports deliver weekly without anyone asking.

Role
Solo builder
Where
TimelinesAI · WhatsApp/CRM software
Tools
Make.com · TypeScript · Close CRM · PostHog · OpenAI · Zoom · GitHub Actions · Slack

Situation

CRM data rots fast. Leads signed up, attended demos, converted, or churned, and the CRM reflected none of it unless someone updated records by hand. Demo insights lived in people's heads. Every week, answering "what's working?" meant hours of manual data pulling. The result was stale data, missed follow-ups, and no clear view of which channels drove revenue.

The decision

Made CRM data and attribution trustworthy enough to actually guide GTM decisions, instead of guessing from stale records.

The system

  1. 1

    Real-time events

    Product events (signup, purchase, churn) automatically update lead status, populate UTM data, and notify Slack, so no human touches the record.

  2. 2

    Demo intelligence

    Demo recordings upload to storage, AI extracts structured insights (pain points, team size, CRM, objections, next steps), and writes a formatted note into the CRM within minutes.

  3. 3

    Reply filtering

    Outbound replies get AI-classified as human vs. automated. Only human replies create CRM notes and alerts; out-of-office and bounces are filtered silently.

  4. 4

    Attribution reports

    A weekly job applies marketing and sales attribution models to every new customer and delivers a formatted Slack report each Monday.

Results

  • Zero manual CRM updates across the full lead lifecycle (trial → customer → churn)
  • Demo insights captured in the CRM within minutes of a call ending
  • Weekly attribution reports delivered automatically, with no more recurring "what's working?" pulls
  • AI reply filtering removed out-of-office and bounce noise from the sales team's view

Under the hood

Make.com, TypeScript, Zod, GitHub Actions, Close CRM, PostHog, OpenAI, Zoom, Slack, Intercom
