AI Cold-Calling System with Circuit Breaker

An AI voice system that makes 100+ calls daily, qualifies leads on the phone, and automatically shuts itself down if anything goes wrong.

Role
Solo builder
Where
TimelinesAI · WhatsApp/CRM software
Tools
n8n · Retell AI · Google Sheets · Slack

Situation

Email and LinkedIn take days to weeks for replies; phone connects instantly. But nobody on a small team can make 100+ cold calls a day while running campaigns, and automated calling is dangerous because one bug can over-call leads, burn numbers, or run up telephony costs with no one watching. The system had to be fully autonomous and provably safe.

The decision

Tested phone as a faster feedback channel without risking runaway automation. Safety was a design requirement, not an afterthought.

The system

  1. 1

    Outbound engine

    Runs hourly on weekdays in each lead's timezone, pulling only eligible leads and initiating AI voice calls with capped attempts and 24-hour cooldowns.

  2. 2

    Post-call qualification

    After each call, AI decides whether a real conversation happened, extracts qualification data, updates status, and alerts sales in Slack for qualified leads.

  3. 3

    Watchdog circuit breaker

    Every 30 minutes it cross-references call logs against the lead database and auto-kills the system on over-calling, re-calls of terminal leads, mass failures, or data drift.

  4. 4

    Callback handler

    When a lead calls back, the system looks up their context and responds with a personalized AI agent.

Results

  • Processed 2,200+ leads in a single campaign
  • Zero watchdog violations: no runaway calls, duplicates, or over-calling
  • Ran fully autonomously, five days a week, with no human intervention
  • Real-time qualified-lead alerts so sales follows up within minutes

Under the hood

n8n (6 workflows), Retell AI, Google Sheets, Slack
