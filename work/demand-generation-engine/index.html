Self-Improving Demand Generation Engine

A system that finds who to target, writes the outreach, sends across channels, and uses conversion data to refine targeting each cycle.

Role
Solo builder (marketing team of 3)
Where
TimelinesAI · WhatsApp/CRM software
Tools
Python · Claude · Close CRM · Apollo · Smartlead · HeyReach

Situation

A B2B SaaS team needed enterprise-scale demand generation across email and LinkedIn — with three people. Every step was manual: segmenting meant eyeballing CRM data, copy took hours per segment, importing leads meant spreadsheet gymnastics, and when a campaign ended there was no systematic way to learn what worked. The team had hit a throughput ceiling it couldn't pass without hiring.

The decision

Stopped treating outbound as one-off campaigns and built a feedback loop from converted leads back into targeting and messaging.

The system

  1. 1

    Enrich

    Raw lead exports run through AI processing — normalization, country validation, title parsing, ICP filtering, deduplication. Large files in, clean targeting lists out.

  2. 2

    Import

    Bulk CRM population with intelligent dedup (matches by URL → LinkedIn → email). Handles 100K+ leads without overwriting existing records.

  3. 3

    Discover

    AI compares converted vs. non-converted leads to find why certain segments close, then generates new targeting criteria and validates reach before passing them downstream.

  4. 4

    Write

    AI drafts email sequences per segment — language-aware for single-country lists, spintax for deliverability. A human approves before send.

  5. 5

    Send

    Campaigns run across multiple providers to reduce single-provider risk, with timezone-aware delivery and zero duplicates.

  6. 6

    Learn

    Conversion data flows back to the CRM. The next cycle discovers refined segments from new results — each run gets smarter.

Results

  • Built demand generation from zero to $100K/month in pipeline within 6 months
  • Lifted MQLs 47% through iterative ICP and segment refinement
  • Cut demand generation spend 30% while quality rose
  • Processed 100K+ leads/month across providers with timezone-aware scheduling

Under the hood

Python, Claude, Close CRM API, Apollo, Smartlead, HeyReach, SQLite, GitHub Actions
