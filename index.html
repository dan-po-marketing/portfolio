Strategy translated into working revenue systems

Four systems built end-to-end. Each one starts with a GTM decision, not a tool.

Self-Improving Demand Generation Engine

Decision · Stopped treating outbound as one-off campaigns and built a feedback loop from converted leads back into targeting and messaging.

Finds who to target, writes segment-specific outreach, sends across email and LinkedIn, and uses conversion data to sharpen the next cycle.

  • $100K/mo pipeline from zero
  • +47% MQL lift
  • −30% spend
Read case study

AI SDR for Reply Qualification & Follow-Up

Decision · Protected sales time by letting AI handle reply triage, qualification, and follow-up — escalating only demo-ready leads to humans.

A multi-agent system that reads replies, classifies intent, qualifies through conversation, drafts responses, updates the CRM, and schedules follow-ups across email and LinkedIn.

  • 1,000+ interactions/mo
  • Half of all sales meetings
  • Sub-minute replies
Read case study

Self-Updating CRM & Attribution System

Decision · Made CRM data and attribution trustworthy enough to actually guide GTM decisions, instead of guessing from stale records.

Product events update CRM statuses, demo recordings become structured notes, replies get classified, and weekly attribution reports show which channels create customers.

  • Zero manual CRM updates
  • Demo insights in minutes
  • Weekly attribution, automated
Read case study

AI Cold-Calling System with Circuit Breaker

Decision · Tested phone as a faster feedback channel without risking runaway automation — safety was a design requirement, not an afterthought.

An AI voice operation that calls during each lead's business hours, qualifies conversations, alerts sales, handles callbacks, and shuts itself down if anomaly checks fail.

  • 2,200+ leads processed
  • Zero watchdog violations
  • 100+ calls/day capacity
Read case study
